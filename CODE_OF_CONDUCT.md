# Community conduct

LimitBar welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give clear reproduction steps and explain technical disagreements.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's private information.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep collaboration productive. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content; do not repeat private information in a public report.
